# Simulated Policy: Loan Eligibility Criteria by Loan Type

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Overview & Category Eligibility Rules
Cred members applying for unsecured Personal Loans must be Indian residents aged 21 to 58 years with a verified minimum net monthly income of INR 35,000 and an active credit history of at least 24 months. Home Loan applicants require co-applicant income verification, a minimum CIBIL score of 720, and continuous employment for at least three consecutive years. Auto, Business, and Education Loans require verified revenue or admission confirmation alongside a creditworthy co-borrower guarantee.

## 2. Exceptions & Escalation Guidance
Applicants falling marginally short of minimum income thresholds may qualify if an eligible family co-applicant is added, provided the total debt-to-income ratio remains below 50%. Applications with non-standard employment or documentation must be escalated to the Credit Underwriting Operations Desk for manual scrutiny.
