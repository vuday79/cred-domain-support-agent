# Simulated Policy: Credit-Score Impact Factors

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Core Scoring Components & Weightings
A member's CIBIL credit score is evaluated on a 300 to 900 scale driven by five distinct credit performance dimensions determined by credit bureau scoring models. Repayment history constitutes the largest weighting factor at 35%, meaning any delayed or missed EMI or card payment significantly depresses the overall score. Credit utilization accounts for 30%, with members advised to maintain revolving card balances below 30% of aggregate sanctioned credit limits to protect their score.

## 2. Balance Factors & Bureau Dispute Escalation
The remaining 35% of the score is governed by credit history longevity (15%), credit portfolio mix (10%), and recent hard credit inquiries (10%). Members identifying inaccurate delinquency notations or fraudulent trade lines must be escalated to the Credit Bureau Dispute Desk for expedited grievance rectification.
