# Simulated Policy: Interest-Rate Slabs

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Credit-Linked Interest Rate Tiers
Cred applies a risk-calibrated interest rate tiering structure anchored to the applicant's CIBIL score and verified debt-to-income ratio. Prime applicants with CIBIL scores of 780 and above qualify for Tier-1 preferential annual rates ranging from 8.5% to 11.25%. Borrowers with scores between 720 and 779 receive Tier-2 rates spanning 11.5% to 15.5%, while scores between 680 and 719 receive Tier-3 rates from 16.0% to 21.0% per annum.

## 2. Rate Revision Exceptions & Sub-Prime Escalation
Floating interest rates are reviewed quarterly in alignment with RBI repo rate adjustments, with revised repayment schedules notified 30 days in advance. Applicants presenting credit scores below 680 are ineligible for standard unsecured facilities and must be escalated to the Risk Review Committee.
