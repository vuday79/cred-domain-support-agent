# Simulated Policy: NRI-Account Eligibility

> [!NOTE]

## 1. NRE vs. NRO Accounts & Repatriation Rules
Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) are eligible to open Non-Resident External (NRE) and Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) accounts through Cred's international banking division. NRE accounts are designated exclusively for foreign income, where deposits are maintained in Indian Rupees with interest income completely tax-free and fully repatriable abroad without limit. NRO accounts facilitate managing legitimate rupee income earned within India, subject to an annual repatriation ceiling of USD 1 million and applicable withholding taxes.

## 2. Onboarding Documentation & FEMA Escalation
Mandatory documentation includes a valid Indian passport, overseas visa or work permit, foreign address proof, and continuous foreign bank statements for the past 3 months. Cross-border inward remittances encountering regulatory documentation discrepancies must be escalated to the Treasury and FEMA Compliance Operations Desk.
