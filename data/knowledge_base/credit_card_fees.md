# Simulated Policy: Credit Card Fee Structure

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Primary Fee Structure & Grace Window
Cred Credit Cards carry a standard annual membership fee of INR 1,500, which is automatically waived if the member achieves cumulative retail spends exceeding INR 1,50,000 in the preceding year. Cardholders receive an interest-free grace window of up to 45 days on retail purchases, after which unpaid balances accrue finance charges at 3.49% per month (41.88% annualized APR). Cash advance withdrawals at domestic or international ATMs attract a transaction fee of 2.5% (minimum INR 500), while foreign currency transactions incur a 1.99% forex markup plus GST.

## 2. Waiver Exceptions & Billing Dispute Escalation
Discretionary fee waivers for technical payment failures may be approved by Customer Support Leads. Cardholders contesting disputed billing charges must be escalated to the Card Billing Disputes Unit within 30 days of statement generation.
