# Simulated Policy: Joint-Account Rules

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Account Operation Mandates & Co-Borrower Liability
Cred joint accounts may be opened by up to three primary individuals operating under Either-or-Survivor, Jointly, or Former-or-Survivor mandates. All applicants must individually complete digital KYC authentication and execute joint mandate declarations prior to account activation. For loan products secured via joint accounts, co-applicants share equal joint and several liability for full repayment of principal and interest dues.

## 2. Unanimous Modifications & Survivorship Escalation
Operating mandate alterations, account closure, or nominee changes require unanimous written consent and authentication from all surviving joint account holders. In the event of a joint holder's demise under an Either-or-Survivor mandate, full operational rights transfer to the survivor, while estate disputes must be escalated to the Legal Banking Cell.
