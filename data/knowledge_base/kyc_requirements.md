# Simulated Policy: KYC Document Requirements

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Mandatory Identity & Address Documentation
In compliance with RBI Know Your Customer (KYC) guidelines, all loan applicants must submit verified Officially Valid Documents prior to loan sanction and disbursement. Mandatory identity verification requires a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) card alongside an Aadhaar card with masked QR code, valid Passport, or Voter ID. Address verification requires utility bills dated within the last 60 days, 6 months of bank statements, or registered rental agreements, followed by digital video-KYC within 72 hours.

## 2. Non-Standard Submissions & Compliance Escalation
In-person physical verification visits may be scheduled if the applicant does not possess an Aadhaar-linked mobile number for instant OTP authentication. Incomplete, damaged, or mismatched KYC documents must be escalated directly to the Compliance and AML Review Cell.
