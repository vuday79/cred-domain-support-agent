# Simulated Policy: Fraud-Dispute Resolution Process

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Incident Reporting SLA & Account Protection
Members suspecting fraudulent transactions or unauthorized loan applications must notify Cred customer support within 24 hours of receiving the unauthorized alert. Customer support immediately suspends digital credentials and places a protective freeze on linked credit lines and auto-debits to prevent subsequent losses. The Fraud Dispute Operations Team investigates incidents within an SLA of 7 to 10 business days, reviewing IP audit trails, device telemetry, and OTP authentication logs.

## 2. Dispute Credits & Redressal Escalation
Provisional dispute credits are credited within 5 business days pending formal forensic verification in accordance with RBI customer liability regulations. Unresolved dispute appeals or disputed forensic conclusions must be escalated directly to the Principal Nodal Officer for administrative review.
