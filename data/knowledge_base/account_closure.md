# Simulated Policy: Account-Closure Process

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Closure Pre-Conditions & Settlement Rules
A Cred savings account or credit line may be closed upon member request, provided there are no pending dues, unbilled card transactions, or ongoing dispute investigations. Members initiate closure requests securely via the authenticated mobile application and settle all outstanding principal and interest dues in full. Once confirmed, all active auto-debits, recurring payment mandates, and digital cards are terminated within 2 business days.

## 2. NOC Issuance & Negative Balance Escalation
A formal No Objection Certificate (NOC) and full closure confirmation letter are issued electronically to the member's registered email within 5 business days. Closure requests encountering un-cleared negative balances or technical mandate cancellation failures must be escalated to the Banking Operations Resolutions Desk.
