# Simulated Policy: Prepayment-Penalty Rules

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Prepayment & Foreclosure Terms by Facility Type
In accordance with Reserve Bank of India regulatory directives, floating-rate retail loans sanctioned to individual borrowers carry zero prepayment or foreclosure penalties. Fixed-rate retail loans foreclosed or partially prepaid within the first 12 months from disbursement incur a prepayment penalty of 2.0% of the prepaid principal sum. After 12 months of regular installment payments, prepayment charges for fixed-rate facilities reduce to 1.0% of the prepaid amount.

## 2. Partial Prepayments & Clearance Escalation
Partial principal prepayments are permitted without penalty up to twice per financial year provided each prepayment amount exceeds three regular EMI values. Requests for immediate full foreclosure requiring collateral release must be routed to the Loan Servicing Operations Desk for final clearance.
