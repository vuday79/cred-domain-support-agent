# Simulated Policy: Minimum-Balance Requirements

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. Average Monthly Balance (AMB) Thresholds
Cred Smart Savings accounts require account holders to maintain an Average Monthly Balance (AMB) of INR 10,000 across metropolitan and urban branches, or INR 5,000 in semi-urban and rural locations. Average Monthly Balance is computed by summing the end-of-day ledger balances across all calendar days of the month and dividing by the total number of days in that month. Account holders falling below the mandated threshold receive automated balance shortfall warning notifications on the 15th and 25th of the billing cycle.

## 2. Shortfall Penalty & Salary Account Exceptions
If the shortfall is not cured by month-end, a non-maintenance penalty charge of INR 250 plus applicable statutory taxes is debited on the first day of the following month. Corporate salary accounts are exempt from minimum balance rules, while genuine hardship fee waiver appeals must be escalated to the Customer Support Team Lead.
