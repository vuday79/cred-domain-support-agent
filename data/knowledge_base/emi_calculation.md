# Simulated Policy: EMI Calculation Rules

> [!NOTE]
> **Simulated Policy Disclaimer:** This document contains simulated banking policies created solely for the Cred Domain Support Agent academic certification capstone. These policies do not represent real-world terms, products, or guidelines of CRED or any affiliated financial institution.

## 1. EMI Computation Methodology & Amortization
Monthly Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs) across all Cred lending products are calculated using the reducing balance method based on the principal amount, tenure, and applicable annual interest rate. The repayment schedule is amortized so earlier installments allocate a higher interest proportion, which systematically transitions toward principal liquidation as the balance declines. Borrowers may select tenures from 6 to 60 months, with payments auto-debited on the 5th calendar day of each month via registered NACH or UPI auto-pay channels.

## 2. Default Grace Period & Exceptions
If an auto-debit attempt fails due to insufficient balance, an automated 3-business-day grace period is provided before late payment fees apply. Requests for EMI restructuring or repayment re-amortization due to financial hardship must be escalated to the Lending Operations Committee.
